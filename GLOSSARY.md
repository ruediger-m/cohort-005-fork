# Cadence

A course platform where learners buy courses and work through their lessons.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's written ask to get their money back for a purchase. It is reviewed by hand and is either Requested, Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel my purchase, reversal, chargeback

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner when their Refund Request is approved. It is always the amount they actually paid, and approval also withdraws their access to the course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, chargeback, cancellation

**Refund Window**:
The period, counted in whole calendar days from the purchase, in which a learner may make a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window

**Chargeback**:
A payment dispute a cardholder raises through their card issuer, outside the platform. Reserved for that meaning only; never a learner's Refund Request.
